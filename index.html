SPOT-AC: Subspace Optimal Transport for Training-Free Zero-Shot Accent Conversion

Audio Demo Page

Anonymous Authors
Under Review

Abstract

Zero-shot accent conversion is challenging because the system should modify pronunciation while preserving linguistic content and speaker traits. Many existing approaches rely on large generative models and expensive training pipelines. We present SPOT-AC, a training-free method that edits frozen WavLM frame embeddings instead of training a new voice conversion model. The method is based on a simple idea: accent-related variation can be edited in an accent-sensitive subspace with limited changes to the full representation. We learn this subspace with a one-time linear calibration, reduce its overlap with gender, and estimate a target-accent shift with unbalanced optimal transport against a target-accent bank. The shifted embeddings are then refined by retrieval and resynthesized with a frozen vocoder. Experiments on L2-ARCTIC show that SPOT-AC improves target-accent accuracy and ASR transcript consistency over accent-aware baselines, while retaining strong speaker resemblance.
